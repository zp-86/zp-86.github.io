PROJECTS

ClosingLine                                          closingline.app
Co-founder · January 2025 to now · Live on iOS and web, public beta

  Sports picks, backed by a model. Pick NHL and MLB games, build parlays
  with free virtual Points, and see how a prediction model rates every
  pick before you make it. Your bets update live as the games play out.

  Co-founded with Dylan Plut (CEO) and Brenden Auger (CMO). I built the
  iPhone app, the website and the server they both run on.

  Stack: SwiftUI, Next.js, React, Tailwind, Python, Flask, Postgres,
         Redis, Docker, Google Cloud, Stripe, Gemini
  ~22,000 lines of Swift in the iPhone app.
  Betting 100 Points on its top pick every night of the 2024 NHL season,
  the model finished +1,587 Points.


Tulip                                                    tulipmap.ca
Co-founder · May 2026 to now · Invite-only beta

  Your travel, on Earth. A 3D globe for everywhere you've been and

  Co-founded with Brenden Auger. I built the website, the iPhone app and
  the server behind them.

  Stack: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Mapbox, Python, FastAPI, Postgres,
         Supabase, SwiftUI
  900+ commits since May 2026.


Circuit Board

Forza Telemetry                     github.com/zp-86/forza-telementry
Open source · February 2026

  Lap analysis for Forza Motorsport. The game streams live data from
  your car while you drive. This tool reads it, draws every lap on the
  track and shows where you gained or lost time against your best.
  My best lap on Maple Valley: 1:24.506, in a Hyundai Elantra N.

  Stack: Node.js, WebSockets, Next.js, React, Tailwind
