About

I'm Zander Plut. I'm in Grade 12 in Toronto, and I build software.

I started with small web projects: a page for chatting with GPT-4, a front end for DALL-E, a Discord bot. They taught me how the pieces of an app fit together, and each project since has been a little bigger than the last.

In January 2025 I co-founded ClosingLine with Dylan Plut and Brenden Auger. Dylan builds the prediction models. I build the iPhone app, the website and the server that connects them to his models.

In May 2026 Brenden and I started Tulip, a travel app built around a 3D globe. I've written the website, the server and the iPhone app.

Outside of those, I like hardware and cars. I designed a circuit board for a school project and wrote a tool to analyze my laps in Forza Motorsport.
