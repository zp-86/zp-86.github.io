  4. Open source · February 2026

    Forza Telemetry

    Lap analysis for Forza Motorsport.

    Forza streams live data from your car while you drive. A small Node server listens for those packets and rebroadcasts them over a WebSocket. The Next.js dashboard draws every lap on the track, splits it into mini-sectors and lays laps side by side, so you can see which corners cost you time.
